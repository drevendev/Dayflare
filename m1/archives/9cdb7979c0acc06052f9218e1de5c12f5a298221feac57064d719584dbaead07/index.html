How the weekly change is measured

Cards compare the last complete seven days with the median of the four preceding complete seven-day totals. A larger share is not proof of a cause. Choose a title to inspect its 35 exact daily observations and evidence.

Order by exact last-week change in requests (last week minus the four-week median), not by importance or Wikipedia-wide popularity. Relative ordering divides that exact signed difference by the four-week median, without rounding. Zero baselines are unavailable and sort last in both directions; tiny baselines can magnify percentages. No thresholds or lifecycle classes are inferred.
