What was measured?

All 20 original API bodies matched byte-for-byte; each card gives the corresponding SHA-256. Identifiers are (project,page_id), not titles. These two snapshots cannot establish changes between sample times or uninterrupted identity, and observed QIDs do not prove cross-language equivalence (Cinema and Football differ).
