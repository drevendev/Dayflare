Volume is not acceleration

Dayflare shows the latest complete seven-day total, the median of four preceding complete seven-day totals (11 August–7 September), and their absolute and baseline-relative differences. A small denominator can exaggerate a percentage; a zero baseline has no meaningful relative percentage.

The selection uses Wikimedia Analytics pageview requests with all-access, user and daily filters. agent=user does not establish that all requests came from people. Counts are neither unique visitors nor approval, truth, importance or causes.

02 / COVERAGE

What is not known

Six preselected EN/RU series across science, literature and chess demonstrate the calculation, not Wikipedia-wide discovery. Missing or unmeasured articles are unknown, not zero. English/Russian editions are not national populations or automatically equivalent entities.

The retained experiment has no stable page-ID observations, so it does not demonstrate redirect, rename or cross-snapshot identity continuity. There are no validated rising, returning or fading lifecycle labels.

Original raw HTTP response bodies are not preserved. The included receipt records provider response metadata, but its reported upstream hashes cannot be recomputed from the normalized JSON alone. Collection occurred before a later Content-Length framing guard; this sample cannot independently prove complete original bodies. A successful local checksum proves only consistency with retained artifacts, not a newly verified Wikimedia response.
