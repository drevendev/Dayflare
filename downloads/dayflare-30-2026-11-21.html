How the weekly change is measured

Cards compare the last complete seven days with the median of the four preceding complete seven-day totals. A larger share is not proof of a cause. Choose a title to inspect its 35 exact daily observations and evidence.

Order by exact last-week change in requests (last week minus the four-week median), not by importance or Wikipedia-wide popularity. Last-week volume orders by the most recent complete seven-day request total, not by change or a Wikipedia-wide popularity ranking. Week-to-week gain/drop orders by the signed exact difference between the last and immediately prior complete UTC week; these are absolute request counts, not percentages or changes against the median. Largest daily rise orders by the biggest positive difference between two consecutive UTC days in these 35 observations (34 comparisons; first day has no predecessor). Zero means no positive step, not a missing day. Relative ordering divides that exact signed difference by the four-week median, without rounding. Zero baselines are unavailable and sort last in both directions; tiny baselines can magnify percentages. No thresholds or lifecycle classes are inferred. The original editorial selection is preserved without JavaScript.

Filter by the exact signed difference: positive, negative or zero. This is a measured comparison, not a validated rising, returning or fading classification. The separate week-to-week direction filter uses the sign of last complete UTC week minus immediately preceding complete UTC week; the filters can be combined without redefining either metric. The card’s separate latest-week step subtracts the immediately preceding complete UTC week from the last complete UTC week. It is not the four-week median delta and does not establish a trend or cause.
